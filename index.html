About

Client-centred legal counsel

Sethu Law Firm was established in Madurai by Advocate Sidhartha Sethupathy with a simple idea: every client deserves individual attention and a legal strategy built around their needs.

Sidhartha Sethupathy practises across criminal, civil and divorce law, and assists with wills and documentation, land registration, licensing, and service and labour writs — appearing before both the High Court and the District Courts.

His approach is to make the law understandable. Complex legal matters are explained in plain language so that clients know where they stand and what happens next — from the details of a land registration to the sensitive questions of a divorce.
